The language thing is not a nice-to-have

If half your customers switch between English and Tamil without thinking about it, an English-only agent doesn't handle half your calls badly. It handles them not at all, and it does it silently, which is worse.

Getting this right takes more than picking a voice that can pronounce Tamil. The part that understands the caller has to handle both languages together, mid-sentence, and most of the systems this industry is built on simply don't. We worked out which ones do, and we build on those.

Why nobody else is offering you this. We looked at what comparable agencies advertise. Every one we found sells in English, and occasionally Spanish. Not one of them could quote you for a Tamil and English agent that switches mid-order.

What it does through a rush

Takes the order

Reads the menu, handles "no onions", takes the address, reads the whole thing back with the total before hanging up.

Answers the same four questions

Are you open, how long for delivery, do you do that dish, where do I park. The ones that interrupt service and never change.

Books tables

Takes the booking, the covers and the time, and refuses one it can't honour instead of taking it and disappointing someone at the door.

Never engaged, never rings out

Takes several calls at once. The fourth caller on a Friday hears the same thing the first one did.

Writes it down

Every order lands somewhere you can see it while the caller is still on the line. No handwriting, no lost sheet.

Works without a till

Plenty of takeaways run on a pad and a pen. That's fine. There's nothing to integrate with and nothing to replace.
