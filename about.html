About

A doctor, a staffing agency, and a phone that wouldn't stop ringing.

Mission4AI exists because we needed one of these ourselves before we ever thought about selling one.

Mission4AI is the trading name of @4 A Mission Ltd, registered in Birmingham. It's run by Dr Nagarasa Bavananthan, a practising GP who is also the managing director of Medico Partners, a healthcare staffing agency billing around £2 million a year placing GPs, nurses, ANPs and support staff across the West Midlands.

The agency's phone is the reason this company exists. Candidates ring at six in the morning about a shift that starts at seven. Clients ring on a Sunday because somebody hasn't turned up. Consultants spend a third of their week on availability calls and chasing compliance documents that expire on a schedule nobody enjoys tracking. None of that is work that fills a job, and all of it has to happen.

So the first agent we designed was for our own candidate line. Not as a demonstration, but because we wanted it. Everything we sell now started as something we needed.

Why the clinical background changes the product

Most people selling AI phone agents have never worked in the industry they're selling into. That's fine for a restaurant booking. It stops being fine the moment the caller is asking a pharmacy about their medication.

An agent that strays into clinical advice is not a bad customer experience. It's a liability, and the line it must not cross is a clinical judgement rather than a commercial one. Knowing where that line sits is the reason we'll build for a pharmacy and most agencies shouldn't.

The same applies in reverse. We're not going to be the right people for every industry. When a job needs knowledge we don't have, the honest answer is to say so, and we'd rather say it on the first call than three weeks into a build.

How we work

  • We tell you on the first call if we don't think there's money in it for you.
  • You get to break the agent before your customers ever hear it.
  • Phone charges are billed at cost, itemised, never marked up or buried.
  • We don't quote statistics we can't stand behind. Where a number is shaky, we say so on the page.
  • The agent tells callers what it is if they ask. It doesn't pretend to be a person.

Where we work

Birmingham is home, and most of what we've built so far is British. The work isn't limited to the UK, though. A phone agent doesn't care where the phone is, and we'll take on work anywhere the numbers make sense for both sides. Anything involving outbound calling needs the local rules checked first, and we do that before quoting rather than after.
