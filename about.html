About

Built by people who have run the businesses we sell to.

Mission4AI is an experienced team across business, sales and IT. We built phone agents for ourselves before we offered one to anybody else.

Mission4AI is the trading name of @4 A Mission Ltd, registered in Birmingham. Behind it is a small team with three kinds of experience: running businesses where the phone is the front door, selling to the owners of those businesses, and building the software that sits underneath.

That mix is the product. The business side knows what a missed call costs on a normal Tuesday and what a phone agent is allowed to say. The sales side has sat on enough discovery calls to know which problems are worth fixing and which aren't. The IT side builds the agent, wires it into your diary and your systems, and keeps it running.

So the first agents we designed were for lines we ran ourselves. Not as demonstrations, but because we wanted them. Everything we sell now started as something we needed.

Why the background changes the product

Most people selling AI phone agents have never worked in the industry they're selling into. That's fine for a restaurant booking. It stops being fine the moment the caller is asking a pharmacy about their medication, or a recruitment desk about a compliance document.

An agent that strays past what it's allowed to say is not a bad customer experience. It's a liability, and knowing where that line sits in each trade is the reason we build for pharmacies, clinics and recruitment desks when most agencies shouldn't.

The same applies in reverse. We're not going to be the right people for every industry. When a job needs knowledge we don't have, the honest answer is to say so, and we'd rather say it on the first call than three weeks into a build.

How we work

  • We tell you on the first call if we don't think there's money in it for you.
  • You get to break the agent before your customers ever hear it.
  • Phone charges are billed at cost, itemised, never marked up or buried.
  • We don't quote statistics we can't stand behind. Where a number is shaky, we say so on the page.
  • The agent tells callers what it is if they ask. It doesn't pretend to be a person.

Where we work

Birmingham is home, and most of what we've built so far is British. The work isn't limited to the UK, though. A phone agent doesn't care where the phone is, and we'll take on work anywhere the numbers make sense for both sides. Anything involving outbound calling needs the local rules checked first, and we do that before quoting rather than after.
