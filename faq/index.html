Frequently asked questions

Answers to common customer questions

Find answers to common questions about AI receptionists, telephone integration, pricing, GDPR and live demonstrations.

What is an AI receptionist?

An AI receptionist is an intelligent telephone assistant that can have natural conversations with callers, answer questions, take messages, manage appointments and carry out approved business tasks.

Will I need to change my telephone number?

Usually not. In most cases, your existing telephone number and telephone provider can be retained through compatible call-forwarding arrangements.

Can it answer calls 24 hours a day?

Yes. Your receptionist can be configured to answer calls 24 hours a day, seven days a week, or during specific hours that you choose.

Can it support my existing receptionist?

Absolutely. It can answer calls when your team is busy, cover lunch breaks, weekends and evenings, or manage overflow calls.

Can it handle multiple calls simultaneously?

Yes, subject to your telephone system and the agreed concurrent call capacity.

Can it transfer calls to my staff?

Yes. Calls can be transferred to appropriate staff members or departments according to agreed rules.

Can it book appointments?

Yes. Appointment booking, cancellation and rescheduling are possible with compatible calendar or booking systems.

Can it speak different languages?

Multilingual voice agents are possible. Your language requirements can be discussed and tested during the demonstration.

How does the AI know about my business?

We configure your receptionist using approved information about your business, including services, opening hours, frequently asked questions and operational procedures.

What happens if it cannot answer a question?

It can be configured to ask for clarification, take a message or transfer the enquiry to a human rather than provide unreliable information.

Is the service GDPR compliant?

Our service is designed to operate within UK GDPR requirements. A written Data Processing Agreement and appropriate data handling arrangements are established before implementation.

Are calls recorded?

Yes. Callers are informed at the beginning of the conversation. Recording, transcription and retention arrangements are discussed during implementation.

How much does it cost?

Our standard AI Receptionist package is normally £399 per month, with an introductory offer of £299 per month. Other packages are available depending on your requirements.

Can I test it before going live?

Yes. We encourage customers to test their customised receptionist before it begins handling live customer calls.

Can I hear a demonstration now?

Yes. Simply call 0121 819 0003 and speak to Maya. She can connect you to a live demonstration for a pharmacy, medical clinic, plumbing business or restaurant.

How do I get started?

Book a free discovery call with our team. We will discuss your business requirements and recommend an appropriate solution without any obligation to proceed.
