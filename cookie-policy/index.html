Cookie Policy

This website does not set cookies or track visitors.

Last updated: 27 November 2026

The short version

This website does not set cookies, and we do not use analytics, advertising or social media tracking on it. Because of that, you will not see a cookie banner.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website can ask your browser to store on your device. They are often used to remember your preferences or to follow what visitors do across pages and websites. Similar technologies, such as local storage, do the same job.

What this website does

  • No cookies. Our pages do not store cookies or similar files on your device.
  • No tracking. We do not use analytics, advertising pixels or tools that follow you around the web.
  • Fonts and images are served from our own website, not from a third party.
  • Telephone and email links open your own phone or email application. We do not record that you used them.
  • The enquiry form sends us only what you type into it, when you press submit. Our Privacy Policy explains how that information is used.

The company that hosts this website receives the technical information every browser sends, such as your IP address, in order to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. That is not a cookie, and we do not use it to identify you.

If this changes

If we add analytics or any other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this page first and ask for your consent before any such cookie is set.

Controlling cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies from any website in your browser’s settings. This website will work in full with cookies blocked.
