Questions people actually ask

Is the monthly fee the whole monthly cost?

No, and we'd rather say so here than in month two. The fee covers the service. Phone and text charges are yours and are billed at cost on an itemised line, typically a small fraction of the fee. We don't mark them up, which also means your bill doesn't climb every time the agent has a busy week.

What happens if we go over the included minutes?

Additional minutes are 25p each. If you're regularly over, we'll tell you to move up a tier rather than quietly billing you the difference.

Do we have to change our phone system?

No. Keep your number and your provider. Going live is a call-forwarding rule we set up with you.

Is there a contract?

No long contract: leave with a month's notice. Setup is a one-off and it's real work, so it isn't refundable once the agent is built, which is why we'd rather talk you out of it on the first call than take it and disappoint you.

Why are you more expensive than the £49 ones?

Because those sell you a bundle of minutes and a script you write yourself. We build the agent around your trade and your rules, and somebody who has worked in your industry decides what it's allowed to say. If a bundle of minutes is what you need, buy one. We'll tell you when that's the honest answer.

What if it doesn't work?

You'll know before it goes live, because you get the number and a fair attempt at breaking it first. If it can't hold up to you, it isn't going anywhere near your customers.
