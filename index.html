How it goes

  1. A 20 minute call

    You describe what your phone does on a normal day and what happens to the calls nobody catches. If we don't think we can make you money, we say so on that call.

  2. We build it against your real business

    Your hours, your prices, your rules, your systems, your language. Not a template with your name dropped into it.

  3. You ring it and try to break it

    Before it goes anywhere near a customer, you get the number and a genuine attempt to catch it out. Interrupt it, change your mind mid-sentence, switch languages, ask something ridiculous.

  4. It goes live on your number

    Keep your existing line and your existing provider. Going live is a forwarding rule we set up with you, not a new phone system.
