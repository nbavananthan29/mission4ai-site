Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect personal information.

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Mission4AI collects when you visit this website, send us an enquiry, call our telephone number or book a discovery call, and what we do with it.

1. Who we are

Mission4AI is the trading name of @4 A Mission Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 14218216), whose registered office is at Radclyffe House, 66–68 Hagley Road, Birmingham, B16 8PF. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean @4 A Mission Ltd.

For the information described in this policy we are the data controller. You can contact us about it at info@mission4ai.com, on 0121 819 0003, or by post at the address above.

2. If you called one of our customers

We provide AI receptionist services to other businesses. If you telephoned a business and spoke to an AI receptionist that we supply, that business decides how your information is used and is the data controller. We handle the information only on its instructions, as its processor, under a written agreement. Please contact that business first, and see its privacy policy, if you have a question about a call you made to it.

4. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To reply to your enquiry, give you a demonstration and arrange a discovery call. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, and taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To provide our services to you if you become a customer, and to manage our relationship with you. Our legal basis is the performance of our contract with you.
  • To check and improve the quality of our AI receptionists, by reviewing recordings and transcripts of calls to our own number. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing a reliable service.
  • To keep business records and meet legal obligations, for example tax and accounting rules.

We contact you about your enquiry only. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use information from calls or enquiries to train our own AI systems.

5. Who we share it with

We use carefully chosen suppliers to run our service. They handle information only on our instructions and under written agreements. They fall into these groups:

  • voice AI, speech and telephone providers, which carry, record and transcribe calls;
  • automation, email, calendar and document providers, which deliver enquiries, bookings and call summaries to our team;
  • the company that hosts this website;
  • professional advisers, such as accountants and solicitors, where needed.

We may also share information where the law requires it.

6. Transfers outside the United Kingdom

Some of our suppliers process information outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States and the European Economic Area. Where they do, we rely on safeguards recognised under UK data protection law, such as UK adequacy regulations or approved contractual clauses. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

7. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries, bookings and related correspondence: up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a customer.
  • Recordings and transcripts of calls to our own number: up to 12 months.
  • Customer and financial records: for the length of the contract and up to seven years afterwards, as required for tax and accounting.

You can ask us to delete your information sooner, and we will do so unless we have a legal reason to keep it.

8. Keeping information secure

We limit access to personal information to the people who need it, use suppliers that apply recognised security measures, and protect our accounts with strong authentication. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and we will tell you and the regulator where the law requires it if something goes wrong.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong;
  • ask us to delete your information, or to restrict how we use it;
  • object to our use of your information where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • ask for your information in a portable format, where this applies.

To use any of these rights, email info@mission4ai.com. We will reply within one month. There is no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so that we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when what we do changes, and change the date at the top. Please check it from time to time.
